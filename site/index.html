CreeperEyes

Animated OLED eyes for a 3D-printed Frankenstein bust. A radar inside the chest wakes him up when someone walks past, his eyes follow the nearest person, and he groans when they lean in.

5Install the firmware

  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a Windows, Mac or Linux computer.
  2. Plug the ESP32 into the computer with a USB cable that carries data (some charge-only cables don't).
  3. Click Install firmware at the top of this page, pick the port that appears (it usually mentions CP210x or USB to UART), then Install.
  4. If it sits on "Connecting…", hold the BOOT button on the ESP32 until the progress starts.
  5. No port listed? On Windows install the Silicon Labs CP210x driver, unplug and replug, and try again.

6Set up from your phone

  1. Power the monster on. You should hear the groan and see both eyes animate.
  2. On your phone, join the Wi-Fi network CreeperEyes-XXXX. The password is frankenstein.
  3. The settings page opens by itself. If it doesn't, browse to http://192.168.4.1.
  4. Pick the eye style, iris and eyelid colours, brightness and volume. Changes show on the eyes immediately and are saved.
  5. Walk in front of him and watch Radar sees on the page. If his eyes look away from you, switch on Flip left/right; if he looks up at close kids, switch on Flip up/down.
  6. Change the Wi-Fi password before the event so trick-or-treaters can't fiddle with him.

Troubleshooting

Screen stays blackCheck red on 3V3, black on GND and white (RST) on GPIO 5.
Static or garbled imageBlue and yellow are swapped: DIN goes to 23, CLK to 18.
Only one eye worksCheck that eye's orange wire: 17 for the left eye, 4 for the right.
Both eyes show the same thingBoth orange wires are on the same pin.
"Radar sees: not connected"Swap the radar's TX and RX wires. The eyes still run normally without the radar.
No soundCheck the card has an mp3 folder with 0001.mp3, the 1 kΩ resistor is on the RX line, and volume isn't 0.
